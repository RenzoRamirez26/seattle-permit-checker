1. PLAN READING (reports/extraction_eval.md)

Date of the run: 21 November 2026. One run.
Model the code used: claude-sonnet-5-5 (the vision model that reads the plan).
Commands, from the repository root:
  python -m evaluation.evaluate_extraction --vision
      Read the three test plans with the model (three model calls) and saved
      each reading.
  python -m evaluation.evaluate_extraction --saved --label "vision model claude-sonnet-5-5, run on 21 November 2026 (three model calls); scored from the saved readings"
      Scored those saved readings and wrote the report. No model call.

Results:

                                    checked correctly  problems          to confirm          alarms

The four misreads, as listed in the report:
  Café with planted problems
    - Misread kitchen: hand_sinks: read None, should be 0
    - Misread fire_safety: exit_signs_shown: read None, should be 0
    - Misread fire_safety: fire_extinguishers_shown: read None, should be 0
  Hair salon with planted problems
    - Misread Door 1: swing: read 'outward', should be 'inward'

The two problems flagged to confirm instead of caught, as listed in the report:
    - Missing exit signs
    - No handwashing sink in the food prep area

How scoring works: each fact the model read is compared with a hand-written
answer key for the plan (areas must be within 2 percent, widths and counts must
match exactly), and the same rules are then run on the model's reading and on
the answer key to count which real problems were caught, flagged to confirm,
missed, and which were false alarms.

Limits: one run, on three invented test plans drawn to be easy to read, with
dimensions printed on them. Real drawings have not been tested.


2. SPOKEN QUESTIONS (reports/voice_eval.md)

Date of the run: 20 November 2026. One run, on one laptop (CPU only).
Command: python -m evaluation.evaluate_voice
Models: Kokoro (text to speech, voice af_heart) spoke ten typical owner
questions; faster-whisper base.en (speech to text) transcribed them.

Results:
  - 9 of 10 questions came back word for word.
  - 10 of 10 would get the same answer from the agent.
  - Speaking a question took 0.8 s on average and transcribing it 0.7 s.
  - The one difference: "Is my business allowed in this zoning?" was heard as
    "Is my business allowed in the zoning?"
  - With the list of code words given to the speech model: 9 of 10 as spoken,
    8 of 10 with hiss added. Without it: 9 of 10 as spoken, 7 of 10 with hiss.

Limits: the questions were machine-spoken, in a clear voice with no accent or
background noise. Real voices will do worse and have not been measured. Ten
questions is a small sample.
